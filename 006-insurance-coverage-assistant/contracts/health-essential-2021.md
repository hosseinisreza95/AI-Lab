# Health Essential 2021 — Policy Wording

> Entry-level generation, closed to new business since 1 January 2024. Members who
> did not migrate to Health Premium 2024 remain covered under these terms.

### ART-1.1 — Purpose of cover

This contract reimburses the insured member and their declared dependants for
medical expenses remaining payable after settlement by the statutory health
insurance scheme, within the limits of this wording.

### ART-1.2 — Dependants

A spouse or civil partner declared on the membership form, and dependent children
up to their 21st birthday where they remain in full-time education.

### ART-2.1 — Hospitalisation — accommodation

Accommodation costs are reimbursed at 100% of the statutory tariff. Costs above
the statutory tariff remain payable by the member.

### ART-2.2 — Hospitalisation — private room

A private room is reimbursed up to 45 EUR per night for a maximum of 20 nights per
calendar year.

### ART-2.4 — Surgical fees

Surgeon and anaesthetist fees are reimbursed at 150% of the statutory tariff for
a practitioner who has signed the fee-moderation agreement, and at 100% otherwise.

### ART-3.1 — Consultations — general practitioner

Reimbursed at 100% of the statutory tariff within the coordinated care pathway.
Consultations outside the coordinated care pathway are not reimbursed.

### ART-3.2 — Consultations — specialist

Reimbursed at 100% of the statutory tariff within the coordinated care pathway.

### ART-3.3 — Teleconsultation

Not covered under this contract.

### ART-4.1 — Optical — frames

One pair of frames per 24-month period, reimbursed up to 50 EUR.

### ART-4.2 — Optical — lenses

Reimbursed up to 40 EUR per simple lens and up to 80 EUR per complex lens, once
per 24-month period.

### ART-4.3 — Optical — contact lenses

Covered only where medically prescribed following intolerance to spectacles,
reimbursed up to 60 EUR per calendar year.

### ART-5.1 — Dental — routine care

Reimbursed at 100% of the statutory tariff.

### ART-5.2 — Dental — prostheses

Reimbursed at 150% of the statutory tariff, capped at 400 EUR per calendar year.
A waiting period of 12 months applies.

### ART-5.3 — Dental — orthodontics

Not covered under this contract.

### ART-6.1 — Hearing aids

Reimbursed up to 200 EUR per ear per 4-year period. A waiting period of 12 months
applies.

### ART-7.1 — Alternative medicine

Not covered under this contract.

### ART-8.1 — Waiting periods

Waiting periods apply as stated in ART-5.2 and ART-6.1. No waiver is available.

### ART-9.1 — Exclusions

The following are not reimbursed:

- treatment that is purely cosmetic;
- thermal spa stays;
- expenses incurred outside the territorial scope in ART-9.2;
- treatment received before the effective date of cover.

### ART-9.2 — Territorial scope

Expenses incurred domestically only. No cover applies outside the national
territory, including for emergency treatment during a temporary stay abroad.

### ART-10.1 — Claim submission

Claims must be submitted within 12 months of the date of treatment.
