# Income Protection 2025 — Policy Wording

### ART-1.1 — Purpose of cover

This contract pays a daily indemnity to the insured member during a period of
total or partial incapacity for work resulting from illness or accident, subject
to the waiting period in ART-3.1 and the exclusions in ART-6.

### ART-1.2 — Persons covered

The named member, provided they were actively at work on the effective date of
cover. Cover for a member who was on sick leave on the effective date begins only
once they have returned to work for a continuous period of 30 days.

### ART-2.1 — Definition of total incapacity

Total incapacity means the member is medically unable to carry out any part of
their own occupation, as certified by a treating physician, and is not engaged in
any other paid activity.

### ART-2.2 — Definition of partial incapacity

Partial incapacity means the member is able to carry out their occupation at a
reduced rate of between 20% and 66% of their normal working time, as certified by
a treating physician and accepted by the insurer's medical adviser.

### ART-2.3 — Consolidation

Consolidation is the date from which the member's condition is no longer expected
to improve. Indemnity under this contract ceases at consolidation. Any permanent
impairment is handled under ART-5 and not under this article.

### ART-3.1 — Waiting period

Indemnity is payable from the 91st day of continuous incapacity for illness, and
from the 31st day for incapacity resulting from an accident. Days of incapacity
separated by a return to work of less than 15 days are treated as continuous for
the purpose of this article.

### ART-3.2 — Reduced waiting period option

Where the member has subscribed the reduced waiting period option recorded in the
schedule, the waiting period is 31 days for illness and 4 days for accident. This
option is not available to members over 55 at subscription.

### ART-4.1 — Amount of the daily indemnity

The daily indemnity equals 1/365th of the insured annual amount recorded in the
schedule, reduced by any daily allowance paid by the statutory social security
scheme for the same period.

### ART-4.2 — Partial incapacity indemnity

During partial incapacity the daily indemnity is paid pro rata to the certified
reduction in working time, with a minimum of 30% and a maximum of 80% of the full
daily indemnity.

### ART-4.3 — Maximum period of payment

Indemnity is payable for a maximum of 1,095 days per claim. Successive periods of
incapacity arising from the same cause and separated by less than 6 months of
continuous work count as a single claim.

### ART-4.4 — Indexation

The insured annual amount is revised on 1 January each year in line with the
published social security ceiling, unless the member has declined indexation in
writing.

### ART-5.1 — Permanent impairment

Where the member's condition is consolidated with a permanent functional
impairment rate of 33% or more, a permanent impairment annuity replaces the daily
indemnity from the consolidation date. The rate is assessed by the insurer's
medical adviser using the published impairment scale.

### ART-5.2 — Review of the impairment rate

The impairment rate may be reviewed at the request of either party, at most once
every 24 months, and in any case not after the member's 62nd birthday.

### ART-6.1 — General exclusions

No indemnity is payable for incapacity resulting from:

- intentional self-inflicted injury during the first 12 months of cover;
- participation in a criminal offence;
- the effects of alcohol above the legal driving limit or of narcotics not
  medically prescribed;
- war, civil war, or active participation in a riot;
- practice of an aviation, motor-racing or high-altitude mountaineering activity,
  unless declared and accepted in writing.

### ART-6.2 — Psychological and psychiatric conditions

Incapacity resulting from a psychological or psychiatric condition is covered,
subject to a specific waiting period of 180 days and to a maximum payment period
of 365 days per claim, unless the condition required hospitalisation of more than
15 consecutive days, in which case the general terms of ART-3.1 and ART-4.3 apply.

### ART-6.3 — Back and spinal conditions

Incapacity resulting from a back or spinal condition without objective imaging
evidence of a lesion is limited to 180 days per claim. Where imaging confirms a
herniated disc, a fracture or a documented degenerative lesion, the general terms
apply.

### ART-6.4 — Pre-existing conditions

A condition diagnosed, treated or investigated in the 24 months before the
effective date of cover is excluded for the first 24 months of cover, unless it
was declared on the membership form and accepted without reservation.

### ART-7.1 — Pregnancy and maternity

Statutory maternity leave is not an insured event. Incapacity arising from a
pathological condition of pregnancy certified by a physician, occurring outside
the statutory maternity leave period, is covered under the general terms with no
specific waiting period.

### ART-8.1 — Claim notification

A claim must be notified within 30 days of the start of incapacity. A late
notification reduces the indemnity by the amount the insurer can show it lost as a
result, and does not by itself void the claim.

### ART-8.2 — Medical certificates

A continuation certificate is required every 30 days. Indemnity is suspended from
the date any certificate expires until a valid certificate is received, and the
suspended period is not recoverable.

### ART-8.3 — Medical examination

The insurer may require an examination by its medical adviser. Refusal without
legitimate reason suspends indemnity from the date of the second written request.

### ART-9.1 — Termination of cover

Cover ends on the member's 67th birthday, on termination of the membership, or on
the date the member ceases to be actively at work other than through an insured
incapacity. Claims in payment at termination continue under the terms in force at
the date of the claim.
