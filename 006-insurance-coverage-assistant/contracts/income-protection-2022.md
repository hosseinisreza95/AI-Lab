# Income Protection 2022 — Policy Wording

> This generation is closed to new business. Members who subscribed before
> 1 January 2025 and did not migrate remain covered under these terms.

### ART-1.1 — Purpose of cover

This contract pays a daily indemnity to the insured member during a period of
total incapacity for work resulting from illness or accident, subject to the
waiting period in ART-3.1 and the exclusions in ART-6.

### ART-1.2 — Persons covered

The named member, provided they were actively at work on the effective date of
cover.

### ART-2.1 — Definition of total incapacity

Total incapacity means the member is medically unable to carry out their own
occupation, as certified by a treating physician.

### ART-2.2 — Partial incapacity

Partial incapacity is not covered under this contract. A member able to work at a
reduced rate receives no indemnity under these terms.

### ART-3.1 — Waiting period

Indemnity is payable from the 91st day of continuous incapacity, whether the
incapacity results from illness or from accident. No reduced waiting period option
is available under this generation.

### ART-4.1 — Amount of the daily indemnity

The daily indemnity equals 1/365th of the insured annual amount recorded in the
schedule, reduced by any daily allowance paid by the statutory social security
scheme for the same period.

### ART-4.3 — Maximum period of payment

Indemnity is payable for a maximum of 730 days per claim.

### ART-4.4 — Indexation

The insured annual amount is fixed at subscription and is not indexed.

### ART-6.1 — General exclusions

No indemnity is payable for incapacity resulting from:

- intentional self-inflicted injury during the first 24 months of cover;
- participation in a criminal offence;
- the effects of alcohol above the legal driving limit or of narcotics not
  medically prescribed;
- war, civil war, or active participation in a riot;
- any aviation, motor-racing, diving or mountaineering activity.

### ART-6.2 — Psychological and psychiatric conditions

Incapacity resulting from a psychological or psychiatric condition is excluded
under this contract, without exception.

### ART-6.3 — Back and spinal conditions

Incapacity resulting from a back or spinal condition is limited to 90 days per
claim, whether or not imaging evidence is available.

### ART-6.4 — Pre-existing conditions

A condition diagnosed, treated or investigated in the 36 months before the
effective date of cover is permanently excluded, unless it was declared on the
membership form and accepted without reservation.

### ART-7.1 — Pregnancy and maternity

Neither statutory maternity leave nor any condition arising from pregnancy is an
insured event under this contract.

### ART-8.1 — Claim notification

A claim must be notified within 15 days of the start of incapacity. A late
notification voids the claim unless the member shows the delay was unavoidable.

### ART-8.2 — Medical certificates

A continuation certificate is required every 30 days.

### ART-9.1 — Termination of cover

Cover ends on the member's 65th birthday or on termination of the membership.
