# Health Premium 2024 — Policy Wording

### ART-1.1 — Purpose of cover

This contract reimburses the insured member and their declared dependants for
medical expenses remaining payable after settlement by the statutory health
insurance scheme, within the limits of this wording and the benefit schedule.

### ART-1.2 — Dependants

A spouse, civil partner or cohabiting partner declared on the membership form, and
dependent children up to their 26th birthday where they remain in full-time
education or are registered as job seekers.

### ART-2.1 — Hospitalisation — accommodation

Accommodation costs in a contracted facility are reimbursed at 100% of the
remaining cost. In a non-contracted facility, reimbursement is capped at 200% of
the statutory tariff.

### ART-2.2 — Hospitalisation — private room

A private room is reimbursed up to 90 EUR per night for a maximum of 60 nights per
calendar year. In a maternity stay the limit is 90 EUR per night for a maximum of
12 nights.

### ART-2.3 — Hospitalisation — companion bed

A companion bed for a parent accompanying an insured child under 16 is reimbursed
up to 45 EUR per night, for a maximum of 30 nights per calendar year.

### ART-2.4 — Surgical fees

Surgeon and anaesthetist fees are reimbursed at 300% of the statutory tariff for
a practitioner who has signed the fee-moderation agreement, and at 200% otherwise.

### ART-3.1 — Consultations — general practitioner

Reimbursed at 200% of the statutory tariff within the coordinated care pathway,
and at 100% outside it.

### ART-3.2 — Consultations — specialist

Reimbursed at 300% of the statutory tariff within the coordinated care pathway,
and at 150% outside it.

### ART-3.3 — Teleconsultation

Reimbursed on the same basis as the equivalent in-person consultation, with no
limit on the number of consultations.

### ART-4.1 — Optical — frames

One pair of frames per 24-month period, reimbursed up to 100 EUR. The period runs
from the date of the last reimbursed purchase, not from the start of the calendar
year.

### ART-4.2 — Optical — lenses

Reimbursed per lens according to the correction class: up to 75 EUR for a simple
lens, up to 150 EUR for a complex lens, up to 200 EUR for a very complex lens, once
per 24-month period. The period is reduced to 12 months for an insured person
under 16, or where a change of correction is prescribed.

### ART-4.3 — Optical — contact lenses

Reimbursed up to 150 EUR per calendar year, whether or not medically prescribed.
This benefit is not cumulative with ART-4.2 in the same 24-month period.

### ART-5.1 — Dental — routine care

Reimbursed at 200% of the statutory tariff, with no annual limit.

### ART-5.2 — Dental — prostheses

Reimbursed at 400% of the statutory tariff, capped at 1,500 EUR per calendar year.
A waiting period of 6 months applies from the effective date of cover.

### ART-5.3 — Dental — orthodontics

For an insured person under 16, reimbursed at 300% of the statutory tariff capped
at 800 EUR per treatment semester. Orthodontic treatment for an insured person of
16 or over is not covered.

### ART-5.4 — Dental — implants

Reimbursed at a flat 400 EUR per implant, for a maximum of 2 implants per calendar
year. A waiting period of 12 months applies.

### ART-6.1 — Hearing aids

Reimbursed up to 800 EUR per ear per 4-year period, in addition to the statutory
reimbursement. A waiting period of 6 months applies.

### ART-7.1 — Alternative medicine

Osteopathy, chiropractic, acupuncture and homeopathy performed by a registered
practitioner are reimbursed at 40 EUR per session, for a maximum of 5 sessions per
insured person per calendar year, across all disciplines combined.

### ART-7.2 — Prevention package

An annual allowance of 150 EUR per insured person covers vaccines not reimbursed
by the statutory scheme, dietician consultations, smoking-cessation support and
sports medical certificates.

### ART-8.1 — Waiting periods

Unless stated otherwise in a specific article, benefits are available from the
effective date of cover with no waiting period. Waiting periods that do apply are
listed in ART-5.2, ART-5.4 and ART-6.1.

### ART-8.2 — Waiver of waiting periods

Waiting periods are waived where the member joins within 3 months of leaving a
comparable group contract, and provides evidence of the previous cover.

### ART-9.1 — Exclusions

The following are not reimbursed:

- treatment that is purely cosmetic and not consecutive to an accident or illness;
- stays in a thermal spa establishment other than the statutory contribution;
- expenses incurred outside the territorial scope in ART-9.2;
- treatment received before the effective date of cover, whatever the invoice date.

### ART-9.2 — Territorial scope

Expenses incurred within the European Union and Switzerland are covered. Outside
that area, only emergency treatment during a stay of less than 90 days is covered,
on the basis of the tariff that would have applied domestically.

### ART-10.1 — Claim submission

Claims settled electronically by the statutory scheme require no action. Other
claims must be submitted within 24 months of the date of treatment.
