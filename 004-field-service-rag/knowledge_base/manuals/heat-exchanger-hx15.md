# Shell-and-Tube Heat Exchanger HX-15 - Manual

## 1. Overview

HX-15 is a single-pass shell, two-pass tube exchanger cooling process gas with
tempered cooling water. Design duty is 6.4 MW with a clean overall heat transfer
coefficient of 780 W/m2K.

## 2. Performance Limits

| Parameter | Normal | Alarm |
|---|---|---|
| Shell-side pressure drop | 0.25 - 0.40 bar | > 0.60 bar |
| Tube-side pressure drop | 0.35 - 0.55 bar | > 0.80 bar |
| Gas outlet temperature | 38 - 45 C | > 52 C |
| Approach temperature | 6 - 10 C | > 16 C |

## 3. Fouling Diagnosis

A rising approach temperature together with a falling pressure drop points to
bypassing (a damaged baffle or a gasket blown between passes), not fouling.

A rising approach temperature together with a rising pressure drop is fouling.
On the water side the deposit is usually scale or biofilm; on the gas side it is
usually polymer or compressor lube oil carry-over.

Calculate the fouling factor before deciding to clean:
Rf = 1/U_actual minus 1/U_clean. Clean when Rf exceeds 0.00035 m2K/W.

## 4. Tube Leak Detection

A tube leak shows as cooling water conductivity rising on the outlet side, or as
water carry-over into the downstream knock-out drum. Isolate, drain, and
hydrotest the tube bundle at 1.5x design pressure to find the leaking tube.
Plugging is acceptable up to 8 percent of total tubes; beyond that the bundle
must be retubed because the remaining tubes see higher velocity and erode faster.

## 5. Cleaning Procedure

Mechanical cleaning (hydroblasting) for the tube side, chemical circulation for
the shell side. Always follow the lockout/tagout procedure and confirm the
exchanger is depressurised, drained, purged with nitrogen, and gas-tested before
opening any flange.
